# AgriCatch Simple User Guide

---

# 1. What is AgriCatch?

AgriCatch is a website that connects you directly with farmers. You can buy fresh agricultural products like vegetables, fruits, and other farm produce straight from the people who grow them.

The platform offers two ways to buy:
- **Available Now** - Products that are ready to ship immediately
- **Pre-order** - Reserve products that will be harvested later

AgriCatch makes it easy for farmers to sell their products and for customers to get fresh, high-quality farm produce.

---

# 2. Who Uses AgriCatch?

## Guest
A guest is someone who visits the website but has not logged in. Guests can:
- Browse products
- Search for items
- View product details
- View farmer profiles
- Read the About page
- Contact support via email

## Customer
A customer is a registered user who buys products. Customers can:
- Browse and search products
- Add items to wishlist
- Add items to cart
- Checkout and pay
- View order history
- Track orders
- Leave reviews
- Send messages to farmers
- Receive notifications
- Create support tickets
- Manage profile and addresses

## Farmer
A farmer is a registered user who sells products. Farmers can:
- Add products to sell
- Choose Available Now or Pre-order mode
- Manage inventory
- View and update orders
- Send messages to customers
- Receive notifications
- Create support tickets
- View sales reports
- Apply for verification
- Subscribe to premium features

## Verified Farmer
A verified farmer is a farmer whose account has been approved by the admin team. Verified farmers have a badge on their profile and their products are more trusted by customers.

## Premium Farmer
A premium farmer is a verified farmer who has subscribed to a paid plan. Premium farmers get extra features like analytics, advanced reports, and priority support.

## Admin
An admin is a staff member who manages the platform. Admins can:
- Approve or reject farmer applications
- Approve or reject products
- Manage orders
- Manage users
- Handle support tickets
- View reports
- Create announcements

## Super Admin
A super admin is the highest level administrator. Super admins can do everything admins can do, plus:
- Manage admin accounts
- Change platform settings
- Change system settings
- View all analytics
- View audit logs

# 3. Guest Journey

### Step 1: Open the Website
Visit the AgriCatch website. You will see the homepage with featured products and categories.

### Step 2: Browse Products
- Click on "Products" in the navigation menu
- You will see a list of all available products
- Each product shows an image, name, price, and farmer name

### Step 3: Search
- Use the search bar at the top of the page
- Type what you are looking for (e.g., "tomato", "vegetables")

- Results will appear below

### Step 4: Filter
- Use the filter options on the side
- Filter by category (e.g., Vegetables, Fruits)
- Filter by price range
- Filter by location
- Filter by farmer

### Step 5: View Product
- Click on any product to see details
- You will see the product image, description, price, and stock
- You can also see the farmer's information
- You can read reviews from other customers

### Step 6: View Farmer
- Click on the farmer's name or profile
- You will see the farmer's shop information
- You can see all products from that farmer
- You can see the farmer's verification status

### Step 7: Contact Us
- Click on "Contact" in the navigation menu
- You will see the contact information
- You can send an email to the support team

- Click the "Login" or "Register" button
- Fill in your information
- After registering, you can access all customer features

# 8. Product Selling Modes

## Available Now
This is for products that are already harvested and ready to sell.

**Example:**
A farmer has 50 kilograms of tomatoes that were harvested yesterday. They list them as "Available Now." Customers can buy immediately, and the farmer ships within 1-3 business days.

**How it works:**
- Farmer adds product with current stock
- Product shows as "In Stock"
- Customer buys and pays
- Farmer ships the product
- Customer receives it quickly

## Pre-order
This is for products that will be harvested in the future.

**Example:**
A farmer is planting corn that will be ready in 2 months. They list it as "Pre-order" with an expected delivery date. Customers can reserve their order now. When the corn is harvested, the farmer ships it to the customers who pre-ordered.

**How it works:**
- Farmer adds product with future harvest date
- Product shows as "Pre-order"
- Customer reserves and pays (or pays a deposit)
- Farmer grows the product
- When harvested, farmer ships to customers
- Customer receives fresh product at the right time

# 9. How Ordering Works

The ordering process follows these steps:

1. **Pending** - The customer has placed the order but it has not been processed yet.

2. **Confirmed** - The farmer has received the order and confirmed it can be fulfilled.

3. **Processing** - The farmer is preparing the order for shipment (picking, packing).

4. **Shipped** - The order has been handed over to the delivery service. The customer can track the shipment.

5. **Delivered** - The customer has received the order. The order is complete.

6. **Cancelled** - The order was cancelled by the customer or the farmer before it was shipped.

7. **Refunded** - The order was returned and the customer received a refund.

**Note:** For Pre-order products, there may be additional waiting time between "Confirmed" and "Processing" while the farmer waits for the harvest.

# 12. Support

If you need help, there are two ways to get support:

### Before Login (Guest)
If you are not logged in, you can contact support via email:
1. Click "Contact" in the navigation menu
2. You will see the support email address
3. Send an email describing your issue
4. The support team will reply via email

### After Login (Registered Users)
If you are logged in, you can create a support ticket:
1. Go to "Support" from your account menu
2. Click "Create New Ticket"
3. Choose a category (e.g., Order Issue, Payment Problem, Technical Issue)
4. Write a detailed description of your problem
5. Attach screenshots if helpful
6. Submit the ticket
7. You will receive updates via notifications
8. You can also view all your tickets and their status

**Response Time:**
- Email support: Usually within 24-48 hours
- Support tickets: Usually within 12-24 hours

1. Log in to your farmer account

3. View available premium plans
4. Choose a plan that fits your needs
5. Complete the payment
6. Your premium features will activate immediately

### How do I buy products?
1. Browse or search for products
2. Click on a product to view details
3. Click "Add to Cart"
4. Go to your cart
5. Click "Checkout"
6. Add or select a delivery address
7. Choose a payment method
8. Confirm your order

### How do I contact support?
- **If not logged in:** Click "Contact" and send an email
- **If logged in:** Go to "Support" and create a support ticket

### How do I track my order?
1. Log in to your account
2. Go to "My Orders"
3. Find the order you want to track
4. Click on the order to view details

### What is the difference between Available Now and Pre-order?
- **Available Now:** Products that are ready to ship immediately
- **Pre-order:** Products that will be harvested in the future; you reserve now and receive later

### Can I cancel an order?
Yes, you can cancel an order if it has not been shipped yet. Go to "My Orders," find the order, and click "Cancel."

### How do I leave a review?
1. Go to "My Orders"
2. Find a completed order
3. Click "Write Review"
4. Rate the product and write your feedback
5. Submit your review

### How do I change my password?
1. Go to "My Profile"
2. Click on "Security" or "Password"
3. Enter your current password
4. Enter your new password
5. Confirm your new password
6. Click "Save"

### How do I add a delivery address?
1. Go to "My Profile"
2. Click on "Addresses"
3. Click "Add New Address"
4. Fill in the address details
5. Click "Save"

### What payment methods are accepted?
The platform accepts various payment methods including credit/debit cards, bank transfers, and digital wallets. The available options are shown during checkout.

### Is my payment information secure?
Yes, all payments are processed through secure payment gateways. Your payment information is encrypted and never stored on our servers.

### How long does delivery take?
Delivery time depends on your location and the product type:
- **Available Now:** Usually 1-3 business days
- **Pre-order:** Depends on the harvest date specified by the farmer

### What if I receive a damaged or wrong item?
1. Go to "Support" and create a ticket
2. Choose "Order Issue" as the category
3. Describe the problem and attach photos if possible
4. Submit the ticket
5. The support team will help resolve the issue

### Can I sell as a farmer without verification?
You can register as a farmer, but your products will not be visible to customers until you are verified. Verification helps build trust with customers.

### How do I delete my account?
1. Go to "My Profile"
2. Click on "Account Settings"
3. Click "Delete Account"
4. Confirm your decision
5. Your account will be permanently deleted
