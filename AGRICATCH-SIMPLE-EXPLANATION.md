# AgriCatch - Simple Explanation (For Everyone)

**What is AgriCatch?**

AgriCatch is like an online market where farmers sell their products directly to customers. Think of it as a farmers' market that you can visit on your phone or computer.

---

## WHO USES AGRICATCH?

**Customers (Buyers)**
- People who want to buy fresh food from farmers
- Can browse products, add to cart, and place orders
- Can track their orders and talk to farmers

**Farmers (Sellers)**
- People who grow food and want to sell it
- Can list their products for customers to see
- Can manage orders and talk to customers

**Admin (Manager)**
- Person who manages the website
- Checks that farmers are real
- Approves products before they appear on the site
- Helps customers and farmers with problems

**Super Admin (Owner)**
- The boss of the website
- Can change any setting
- Has full control of everything

---

## HOW CUSTOMERS USE AGRICATCH

**Step 1: Sign Up**
- Fill in your name, email, and password
- Choose "Customer" as your role
- Click "Register"

**Step 2: Browse Products**
- Look at pictures of vegetables, fruits, and other farm products
- Search for what you want
- Filter by type (vegetables, fruits, etc.)

**Step 3: Add to Cart**
- Click "Add to Cart" on products you want
- See all items in your cart
- Check the total price

**Step 4: Checkout**
- Enter your address for delivery
- Review your order
- Click "Confirm Order"

**Step 5: Wait for Delivery**
- Watch your order status change
- Get notifications when something happens
- Farmer prepares your order
- Farmer delivers your order
- You receive your order

**Step 6: Review**
- After you receive your order, you can rate the product
- You can also rate the farmer
- Write a comment if you want

---

## HOW FARMERS USE AGRICATCH

**Step 1: Sign Up**
- Fill in your name, email, and password
- Choose "Farmer" as your role
- Click "Register"

**Step 2: Get Verified (Optional)**
- Ask the admin to verify you
- Admin checks that you are a real farmer
- Once verified, you get a badge that shows customers you are trusted

**Step 3: Add Products**
- Click "Add Product"
- Upload a photo of your product
- Write the name, price, and description
- Say how many you have available
- Click "Save"

**Step 4: Wait for Approval**
- Admin reviews your product
- Admin checks that it follows the rules
- Admin approves your product
- Now customers can see and buy it

**Step 5: Receive Orders**
- When a customer buys, you get a notification
- You see the order in your dashboard
- You accept the order
- You prepare the product
- You schedule delivery
- You deliver to the customer
- You mark the order as delivered

**Step 6: Get Premium (Optional)**
- Pay to become a premium farmer
- Get a special badge
- Can list more products
- Get more features

---

## HOW ORDERS WORK

**When a Customer Places an Order:**
1. Customer clicks "Confirm Order"
2. Order shows as "Pending"
3. Farmer sees the order
4. Farmer accepts the order → "Confirmed"
5. Farmer prepares the product → "Preparing"
6. Farmer schedules delivery → "Scheduled"
7. Farmer starts delivery → "Out for Delivery"
8. Farmer delivers to customer → "Delivered"
9. Order is finished → "Completed"

**If Something Goes Wrong:**
- Customer can cancel if order is still "Pending"
- Farmer can cancel if order is not yet delivered
- Admin can cancel any order (except finished ones)

---

## HOW PRODUCTS GET ON THE SITE

**Step 1: Farmer Adds Product**
- Farmer uploads photo and details
- Product is hidden from customers

**Step 2: Admin Reviews**
- Admin checks the product
- Admin makes sure it follows rules

**Step 3: Admin Approves**
- Admin clicks "Approve"
- Product becomes visible to customers
- Customers can now buy it

**Step 4: If Not Approved**
- Admin clicks "Reject"
- Product stays hidden
- Farmer gets a message why it was rejected

## WHAT IS PRE-ORDER?

**Regular Order:**
- Product is available now
- You buy it
- Farmer delivers it

**Pre-Order:**
- Product is not available yet
- You reserve it now
- Farmer grows or prepares it
- On the availability date, it becomes a regular order
- Farmer delivers it

**Example:**
- Farmer plants tomatoes today
- Tomatoes will be ready in 2 weeks
- Farmer lists them as pre-order
- You reserve 5 kilos today
- In 2 weeks, farmer harvests
- Farmer delivers your 5 kilos

## WHAT IS PREMIUM?

**Regular Farmer:**
- Can list some products
- Has basic features

**Premium Farmer:**
- Pays a monthly fee
- Can list more products
- Gets a special badge
- Has extra features
- Gets priority in search

**How to Get Premium:**
1. Farmer clicks "Subscribe"

3. Pays (shows proof of payment)
4. Admin reviews payment
5. Admin approves
6. Farmer becomes premium
7. Features unlock

## WHAT ARE NOTIFICATIONS?

**Why You Get Notifications:**
- When something important happens
- When your order status changes
- When someone messages you
- When you get a review
- When there's an announcement

**Types of Notifications:**
- "Your order has been confirmed"
- "Your order is out for delivery"
- "You have a new message"
- "Your product was approved"
- "New announcement from AgriCatch"

**How to See Them:**
- Click the bell icon
- See all your notifications
- Click to mark as read
- Click "Mark All as Read" to clear them

---

## WHAT ARE REVIEWS?

**Why Reviews Matter:**
- Customers can tell others about products
- Farmers can see what customers think
- Helps other customers decide what to buy
- Helps farmers improve

**How to Write a Review:**
1. Only after you receive your order
2. Click "Rate Product"
3. Give 1 to 5 stars (5 is best)
4. Write a comment (optional)
5. Click "Submit"
6. Review is saved
7. Farmer sees your review
8. Other customers can see your review

**Can You Change Your Review?**
- Yes, you can edit it for 1 month
- After 1 month, it cannot be changed

---

## WHAT ARE ADDRESSES?

**Why You Need Addresses:**

**How to Add an Address:**
1. Go to "Account"
2. Click "Addresses"
3. Click "Add Address"
4. Fill in your address details
5. Click "Save"
6. Address is saved
7. You can use it for future orders

## WHAT IS WISHLIST?

**Why Use Wishlist:**

**How to Use Wishlist:**
